Freetime News is a native Android app for reading news, release posts and updates from Freetime Maker.

Posts are loaded through the public Freetime Maker API and displayed directly in the app using a native Jetpack Compose interface. The app does not use a WebView for article content.

Features:
* Browse Freetime Maker news and release posts
* Read Markdown-based articles natively
* View article categories
* View release dates in the device's local date and time format
* Lightweight interface built with Jetpack Compose

An internet connection is required to retrieve the latest posts.
